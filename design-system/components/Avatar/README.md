Avatar is the user's photo, or their initial, on a round image inside a small torn `sheet-50` frame.

**Spec.** The frame has an `xs` tear, 3px padding and seeded ±3° rotation. The image is round with `object-fit: cover`. The default is an initial (Special Elite, 42% of the size, `ink-deep` on `pink-200`), or a person glyph when there is no nickname yet. Sizes are 34 (menu), 40 (masthead) and 72 (sign-up). As a button it is the Radix DropdownMenu trigger with the traced focus ring, and its accessible name is "Account menu for <name>".
