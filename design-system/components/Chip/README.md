Chip is a small torn toggle used for tools, shapes, pattern kinds, tape direction and ends.

**Anatomy.** A `<button>` wrapper (`.zf-torn`, `xs` tear) holding a face (`sheet-50`) with an optional icon, a label (Special Elite 14px), and a check when selected without an icon.

**States.**
- Default: `sheet-50`, `ink`.
- Selected (`aria-pressed`, or `aria-checked` in a radiogroup): `selected` lime with `ink-deep` (7.12:1), plus the icon or check, never colour alone.
- Hover: 0°, −1px, `brightness(.96)`.
- Focus: traced ring.
- Disabled: sage, 60% ink.

Flat throughout. 36px min height, or a 44px hit area on touch.
