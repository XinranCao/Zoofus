Loader covers three loading patterns: inline typing dots, an area tape reel and grid skeletons.

- **Typing.** The verb plus three dots that appear one after another (steps timing). Used in buttons ("Saving…").
- **Tape reel.** A 34px mustard roll spinning slowly at 1.4s per turn, plus a verb. Used in the canvas well and page areas.
- **Skeleton.** `sage-100` scraps at tile size, breathing between 100% and 55% opacity.

All three have `role="status"`. Under reduced motion the dots stay visible and nothing spins or breathes.
