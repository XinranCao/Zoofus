SelectMark is the tick on a tile while a person is picking many things at once (bulk select).

**Anatomy.** A small irregular scrap in the tile's top corner, `aria-hidden` (the tile itself is the `aria-pressed` button). Selected: `selected` lime with a check icon. Not selected: an empty `sheet-50` scrap.

**Rules.** Selected is shown by the check and the colour, never colour alone. It is never a round or square form control.
